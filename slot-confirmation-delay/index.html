Problem statement

When a customer doesn't respond to a proposed slot and the slot day is about to pass — or has already passed — the partner has no clarity about what happens next.

The slot day is gone, but the app still shows the same date. If the customer responds now, will the install just shift by one day? Will the partner's bonus get cut?

With so many unanswered questions, partners often end up cancelling the booking themselves.
